Privacy policy

camera-ingest is a private device used only by its owner's household.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses the Google Photos scope photoslibrary.appendonly to upload photos and videos and to create the albums they go into. This scope cannot read, list, change or delete anything else in the Google Photos library.

What it stores, and where

Photos and the Google sign-in token are stored only on the owner's own device and home storage. Nothing is sent to anyone other than Google Photos, and nothing is sold or shared.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
